# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith, focus criticism on ideas and code, and protect the privacy of users and contributors.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `imrangulhassan@gmail.com`. Maintainers will review reports fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and disclose conflicts of interest. This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project publicly.
